Work awarded the 8th Prize (2013-2014) of the Mathematics Research Initiation Program (PIM) at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (UAM), co-authored with Fernando Ballesta Yagüe and Javier García Román. The work addresses the resolution of optimization problems through the use of inequalities, exploring an alternative approach to classical differential calculus methods.
